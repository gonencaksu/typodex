# Privacy Policy

**App:** typodex
**Developer:** Gönenç Aksu
**Effective date:** October 28th 2026

## Data we collect

We do not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Where your data is stored

Everything you enter stays on your device. If iCloud is enabled, your data syncs through your own private iCloud account, which is managed by Apple. We have no access to it.

## Third parties

We do not share data with anyone. We use no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app and it's folder removes local data. To remove synced data, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage and delete [App Name]'s data.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It contains no content by default, everything in it is created by the user. The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
